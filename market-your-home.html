Marketing Your Home · Utah County

Getting your home seen.

Marketing is what turns a listing into a sale. Here is everything you can do yourself to get your home in front of buyers, and the honest truth about the one tier you can't reach alone.

Most of a buyer's first impression happens on a screen, long before they ever pull up to the curb. So the goal is simple: get your home in front of as many of the right people as possible, looking its best. A lot of that is fully in your hands.

What You Can Do

Market it like it matters.

None of this needs a license. Work down the list and you will already be ahead of most for-sale-by-owner listings.

✓

Professional photos

The single biggest lever. Buyers scroll past dark, crooked phone photos. Hire a real estate photographer, it pays for itself in interest.

✓

A clear, honest listing description

Lead with what makes the home special, name the neighborhood perks, and be specific. Skip the fluffy adjectives.

✓

Yard sign with your number

Neighbors talk, and drive-by buyers are real. A clean sign with a rider and your phone number still works.

✓

The for-sale-by-owner sites

Post to Zillow's for-sale-by-owner path, Redfin, FSBO.com, Craigslist, and Facebook Marketplace. Free reach you control.

✓

Your own network and social

Your circle is your warmest audience. Post it, share it, and ask people to pass it along. Word of mouth still sells homes.

✓

An open house

Pick a weekend, put out signs, and let people walk through. It creates urgency and gets real feet in the door.

The Honest Ceiling

Where doing it alone hits a wall.

You can do a lot. But two of the most powerful places a home gets seen are gated, and it is worth knowing that going in.

The MLS and its reach

When a home goes on the MLS, it fans out automatically to hundreds of sites. On your own, the closest you can get is a paid flat-fee service that drops in a bare-bones MLS entry that you manage yourself. It works, but it is not the same as the full syndication and positioning a listing agent brings.

Zillow Showcase

This one you cannot reach at all on your own. Showcase is available only through a licensed agent, and it is capped at 10% of the listings in any given market. It is the premium, AI-powered listing experience on the most-visited real estate site in the country, and it is the piece I can add to your home that a for-sale-by-owner listing simply cannot have.
